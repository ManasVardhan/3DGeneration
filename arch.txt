Training notes:
• N = 8192 vertices; V0 is a unit icosphere template with fixed topology F.
• DINOv2 is frozen (both CLS descriptors and mid-level maps are used).
• Anneal λ_edge and λ_lap from 0 → target over first ~30 epochs for stability.
• Optimizer: AdamW; warmup+cosine on Stage-1, fixed LR on Stage-2; grad clip 1.0.
